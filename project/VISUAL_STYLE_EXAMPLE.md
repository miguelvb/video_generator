## VISUAL STYLE

### GLOBAL VISUAL IDENTITY
2D watercolor and fine black ink on warm aged cold-press paper. Muted indigo, cobalt, emerald and sepia washes. Hand-drawn technical schematic. Organic imperfect ink contours. Documentary infographic composition. Consistent line weight, paper texture, lighting, palette, iconography and visual density across the entire film. All scenes must look like parts of the same documentary/explainer. No photorealism, neon, glossy CGI or random style changes.

### STYLE REFERENCE
assets/reference/storyboard.png

### CAMERA STYLE
Slow restrained cinematic movement. Subtle push-ins, slow pans and gentle parallax. Locked camera when appropriate. Never handheld, shaky, spinning or randomly zoomed.
